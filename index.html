Beginner Climbing Plan V0–V3

Simple weekly plan with climbing, indoor cycling, walking/running, strength, and progress tracking.

Weekly Training Structure

Beginner level. Main goal: build technique, aerobic base, strength foundation, and avoid finger/shoulder overload.

3 climbing sessions 2 strength sessions 2 conditioning sessions 1 full rest day
Day 1 — Volume Climbing V0–V2 · 45–60 min
  • 20–30 easy problems.
  • 70% V0–V1, 30% V2.
  • Silent feet. Slow movement. No rushing.
Day 2 — Upper Strength + Indoor Cycling Strength 30–40 min · Bike 20–30 min
  • Assisted pull-ups, push-ups, scapular work.
  • Indoor bike in easy Zone 2.
  • No max finger training.
Day 3 — Outdoor Walk / Easy Run 30–45 min
  • Walk fast or jog slowly.
  • You must be able to speak.
  • No hard intervals yet.
Day 4 — Limit Climbing V2–V3 · 45–60 min
  • 3–6 harder problems.
  • 3–5 attempts each.
  • Rest 2–3 min between attempts.
Day 5 — Lower Body + Core 35–45 min
  • Squats, glute bridges, split squats.
  • Plank and side plank.
  • Build heel-hook and body-tension base.
Day 6 — Easy Climb + Walk V0–V1 · 30–45 min + walk 20–30 min
  • Technique only.
  • No hard attempts.
  • Finish feeling fresh.
Day 7 — Full Rest No training
  • Sleep, food, recovery.
  • Light mobility only if needed.
  • Finger pain means more rest.

8-Week Progression

Weeks 1–2: Base

  • Climb mostly V0–V1.
  • Only test V2 when movement is controlled.
  • Bike/walk easy.

Weeks 3–4: Build

  • Add more V2 volume.
  • Try V3 only on limit day.
  • Add 5–10 minutes to easy cardio if recovery is good.

Week 5: Deload

  • Reduce total climbing volume by 30–40%.
  • No hard limit attempts.
  • Keep walking and easy cycling.

Weeks 6–8: Consolidate

  • Make V1 automatic.
  • Make V2 stable.
  • Try V3 with good rest, not tired.
Stop a session if finger pain, elbow pain, shoulder pain, or form breakdown appears. Beginner progress comes from consistency, not destruction.

Exercise Guide

Images use external URLs. For true offline use, download the images later and replace each image URL with a local file path.

Volume Climbing

  • Choose easy routes.
  • Move slowly.
  • Place feet quietly.
  • Do not fight every move.

Limit Climbing

  • Pick V2–V3 problems.
  • Rest properly.
  • Focus on one move at a time.
  • Stop before sloppy attempts.

Indoor Cycling

  • 20–30 minutes.
  • Easy breathing.
  • No sprinting.
  • Used for recovery and aerobic base.

Outdoor Walk / Easy Run

  • 30–45 minutes.
  • Walk fast or jog slowly.
  • Stay conversational.
  • Do not turn it into a hard run.

Assisted Pull-Up

  • 3 sets of 5–8 reps.
  • Full control.
  • No swinging.
  • Use assistance if needed.

Push-Up

  • 3 sets of 8–12 reps.
  • Straight body.
  • Chest moves toward floor.
  • Use knees if needed.

Squat

  • 3 sets of 10–15 reps.
  • Knees track over toes.
  • Controlled depth.
  • No collapsing inward.

Plank

  • 3 sets of 30–45 seconds.
  • Ribs down.
  • Glutes tight.
  • No sagging lower back.
