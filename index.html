Weekly Structure

Beginner plan. Goal: technique, consistency, aerobic base, strength foundation, and injury prevention.

3 climbing sessions 2 strength sessions 2 conditioning sessions 1 full rest day
Day 1 — Volume Climbing V0–V2 · 45–60 min · RPE 4–6
  • 20–30 easy problems.
  • 70% V0–V1, 30% V2.
  • Silent feet. Slow movement. No rushing.
  • Stop before finger or shoulder fatigue.
Day 2 — Upper Strength + Indoor Cycling Strength 30–40 min · Bike 20–30 min · RPE 3–5
  • Assisted pull-ups: 3 x 5–8.
  • Push-ups: 3 x 8–12.
  • Scapular pull-ups: 3 x 8–10.
  • Easy Zone 2 indoor bike.
Day 3 — Outdoor Walk / Easy Run 30–45 min · RPE 2–4
  • Fast walk or very easy jog.
  • You must be able to speak.
  • No intervals yet.
Day 4 — Limit Climbing V2–V3 · 45–60 min · RPE 6–8
  • 3–6 harder problems.
  • 3–5 attempts per problem.
  • Rest 2–3 min between attempts.
  • Stop when movement gets sloppy.
Day 5 — Lower Body + Core 35–45 min · RPE 4–6
  • Squats: 3 x 10–15.
  • Glute bridges: 3 x 12.
  • Split squats: 3 x 8 each side.
  • Plank: 3 x 30–45 sec.
Day 6 — Easy Climb + Walk V0–V1 · 30–45 min + walk 20–30 min · RPE 2–4
  • Technique only.
  • No hard attempts.
  • Finish fresh.
Day 7 — Full Rest No training
  • Sleep, food, recovery.
  • Light mobility only if needed.

8-Week Progression

Weeks 1–2: Base

  • Mostly V0–V1.
  • Only try V2 when controlled.
  • Bike and walk easy.
  • No fingerboard max training.

Weeks 3–4: Build

  • Add more V2 volume.
  • Try V3 only on limit day.
  • Add 5–10 min to cardio if recovery is good.

Week 5: Deload

  • Reduce climbing volume by 30–40%.
  • No hard limit attempts.
  • Keep walking and easy cycling.

Weeks 6–8: Consolidate

  • Make V1 automatic.
  • Make V2 stable.
  • Try V3 only with good rest.
Stop immediately if you feel finger pain, elbow pain, shoulder pain, or sharp joint pain.

Exercise Guide

Volume Climbing

  • Choose easy routes.
  • Move slowly.
  • Place feet quietly.
  • Keep hips close to the wall.

Limit Climbing

  • Pick V2–V3 problems.
  • Rest properly.
  • Focus on one move at a time.
  • Stop before sloppy attempts.

Indoor Cycling

  • 20–30 minutes.
  • Easy breathing.
  • No sprinting.
  • You should be able to talk.

Outdoor Walk / Easy Run

  • 30–45 minutes.
  • Walk fast or jog slowly.
  • Stay conversational.

Assisted Pull-Up

  • 3 sets of 5–8 reps.
  • No swinging.
  • Stop 2 reps before failure.

Scapular Pull-Up

  • 3 sets of 8–10 reps.
  • Keep arms mostly straight.
  • Pull shoulder blades down.

Push-Up

  • 3 sets of 8–12 reps.
  • Straight body.
  • Use knees if needed.

Squat

  • 3 sets of 10–15 reps.
  • Knees track over toes.
  • No knee collapse inward.
